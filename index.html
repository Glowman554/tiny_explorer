About the Project

Tiny Tapeout Explorer is an interactive web-based visualization tool for exploring integrated circuit designs from the Tiny Tapeout community. Our features and motivation include:

  • Features: Lightning-fast Manhattan-optimized layout parsing, hierarchical structural mapping, 3D and 2D viewing, switch-level simulation, and a modular architecture.
  • Motivation: To provide a seamless, high-performance, browser-based environment for examining and sharing silicon designs without complex local tools.

This project serves as a major upgrade to my previous simulator, the original TT09 WebGL GDS Viewer. It builds upon its foundation with faster multi-threaded extraction, deeper interactive capabilities, and native VGA simulation.

Author: Alexander Mordvintsev (znah, moralex)
